# Design System: The Monochrome Studio Suite

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Precision Monochrome Minimalism"**

Engineered for freelance video editors, motion designers, and post-production creators who demand an uncluttered, high-contrast, distraction-free environment. Built around deep OLED true black (`#000000`), razor-sharp whites (`#ffffff`), and calibrated neutral grays (`#a1a1aa`, `#71717a`), the design uses the modern **Inter** font family for crisp legibility and expansive whitespace for a calm, non-crowded experience.

**Core Tenets:**
- **Pure Black & White Contrast**: Crisp typography on deep obsidian surfaces. Zero rainbow accent clutter.
- **Spacious Breathing Room**: Generous padding, reduced visual density, and structured information hierarchy.
- **Inter Typography**: Clean, modern sans-serif paired with tabular figures for exact numerical clarity.

## Palette

- **Pitch Black** (`#000000`): The primary workstation canvas.
- **Panel Black** (`#0a0a0a`): Primary content cards and containers.
- **Elevated Black** (`#121212`): Hover states, modal surfaces, and elevated panels.
- **Track Surface** (`#141416`): Table headers and input wells.
- **Pure White** (`#ffffff`): Primary headings, key metrics, active chart paths, and primary action buttons.
- **Neutral Grays** (`#d4d4d8`, `#a1a1aa`, `#71717a`, `#3f3f46`, `#27272a`): Secondary metrics, chart categories, and borders.

## Typography

- **Interface & Content**: `Inter` (Google Fonts), geometric precision and high scannability.
- **Tabular Numerals & Timecodes**: Monospace figures (`ui-monospace, Consolas, monospace`) for exchange calculations and currency conversions.

## Layout & Components

- **Console Header**: Minimalist bar with `NAHYAN // STUDIO` branding, live timecode clock, and crisp white action button.
- **Spacious Telemetry Rack**: 4 high-impact metrics (Net Earnings, Hourly Yield, Pending Receivables, USD Retainers) with clear margins.
- **Monochrome Revenue Waveform**: White trajectory line with subtle gradient area fill and high-contrast tooltip.
- **Edit Format Distribution**: Monochromatic grayscale breakdown of video disciplines.
- **USD ⇄ INR Switchboard**: Clean dual-currency converter with retainer presets.
- **Production & Overhead Ledger**: High-contrast, spacious table with generous row padding and clear status tags.
